### Hi, I'm Toluwanimi Arogundade, MSW, LSW

I'm a social work researcher moving into data science and AI evaluation in child welfare and behavioral health. My work brings clinical practice, measurement, and data together to answer one question: do the tools and systems we use actually work for children and families?

**Currently**

- Research and Mental Health Counselor at GRO Community, where I led the content validation of a 60-item clinical competency instrument (CVI and modified kappa computed in Python; n=100 clinicians)
- Building a [data portfolio](https://github.com/tarogundade-lab/data-portfolio) on foster care disproportionality, fairness in risk models, and evaluating AI tools for human services
- Ad hoc reviewer for *Social Work in Public Health*

**Research interests:** racial disproportionality in foster care, instrument development and psychometrics, program evaluation, and the responsible use of AI in social services.

**Skills:** Python (pandas, statsmodels, scikit-learn), SQL, psychometrics, mixed methods, qualitative coding, program evaluation
